Chapter 10
Energy Methods
In this chapter, we will learn a new topic Energy Methods which is an alternate method to obtain
deformation of general structures including beams. Unlike stress equilibrium equations solving which we
obtain displacement of every point in a body simultaneously, the energy methods gives displacement of only
few special points in the body. However, as we will see, the new approach is algebraic in nature and much
simpler to solve than partial differential equations. We will first learn about various concepts needed for
understanding energy methods. We will then see how one can apply this method for solving beam
deformation problems.
