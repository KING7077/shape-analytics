Importance of traction
By definition, it gives us the intensity of force with which one part of the body is pulling or pushing the other
part of the body. If the value of this traction is lower than a threshold limit, the body will not fracture/fail. At a

that point would the body fail! For example, it has been experimentally observed during tensile loading of a
